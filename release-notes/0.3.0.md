## What's new in 0.3.0

- **Screen recording from the notch**: record the whole screen, a window or a dragged area, with system audio and/or your mic. A red dot and timer sit beside the notch while recording. ⌃⌥⌘R starts and stops.
- **Screenshots**: screen, window or area (⌃⌥⌘S), saved or copied. NotchPilot never shows up in your captures.
- **6 new themes**: Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Rose, Gold and Aurora (an animated gradient edge).
- **Smoother animations**: a bouncy open, a sliding tab highlight, blur transitions.
- **Bigger, easier-to-click buttons** throughout the notch.
- **New Settings window** with a sidebar, so every module's settings are easy to find.

Recording needs macOS 15 or later and asks for Screen Recording permission. If it stops working after an update, switch NotchPilot off and on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.
