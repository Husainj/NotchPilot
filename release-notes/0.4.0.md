## What's new in 0.4.0

- **Colourful tabs**: every tab now has its own colour, and that colour carries into the tab's sliders, rings and highlights. Prefer one colour? Turn off **Colourful tabs** in Settings → Appearance to use your theme's accent.
- **Tab names**: the selected tab shows its name next to the icon, so you always know where you are. Icons are filled and easier to see.
- **Redesigned System tab**: Volume, Display and Keyboard sliders with a live percentage, plus cards for battery and Keep Awake.
- **Keyboard backlight control**: adjust your keyboard light right from the notch. On Macs with keyboard-light keys, they now show the notch HUD too.
- **Redesigned Stats tab**: colourful cards for CPU, Memory, Disk and Network.
- **Eyes**: a new idle look for the closed notch. Two eyes sit on either side of the notch, follow your pointer and blink now and then. Turn it on in Settings → Appearance → "When nothing is live, show" → Eyes.
- **Hand cursor**: the pointer turns into a hand over anything clickable in the notch.

The keyboard slider only appears on Macs with a backlit keyboard. Showing the HUD for the volume, brightness and keyboard keys still needs Accessibility permission (Settings → System → Replace the HUD).
