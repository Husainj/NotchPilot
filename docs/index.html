What's inside

✱ AI coding control center

Live Claude Code, Codex and Cursor sessions: working, needs approval, done. Usage, cost and when your 5-hour limit resets.

🎙 Meeting mode

Mute your mic in every app with one shortcut, see who's using your mic and camera, and join your next call in one click.

☕ Keep awake

Your Mac stays awake while an agent is working, then sleeps normally again. Manual 30m / 1h / ∞ too.

🎵 Now Playing

Artwork and controls for any app: Spotify, Music, YouTube in the browser, local files.

📥 Shelf & clipboard

Drop files on the notch, drag them out later. Searchable clipboard history that never stores passwords.

⏱ And more

Pomodoro timers, notes, calendar and reminders, weather, system stats, a volume/brightness HUD and a camera mirror.

Private by design

Everything runs on your Mac. No account, no analytics, no tracking. AI usage is read from your local logs and never uploaded. The code is open, so you can check.

FAQ

Is it really free? Yes, free and open source under the MIT license. Contributions are welcome on GitHub.

macOS says it can't verify the app. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Follow the 2-step install guide; you only do it once.

How do updates work? The app checks for updates daily and installs them in one click. Releases are signed, so only real updates are accepted.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue.
